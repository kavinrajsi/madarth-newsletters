## The short answer

Use AI for speed. Keep your point of view human.

## Why distinctiveness matters

When everyone can produce clean, competent creative, the brands that stand out are the ones with a clear voice and a specific cultural reference point.

### What to do this quarter

- Write down your brand's point of view in one sentence.
- Audit your last ten posts for sameness.
- Keep a human editor on every piece of AI-assisted work.
